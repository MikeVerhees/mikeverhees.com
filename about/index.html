About me

Hi! I’m Mike 👋

I am a Software Developer based in Eindhoven, The Netherlands, passionate about building software that is easy to use, secure, and privacy-respecting.

Currently I work at e-Quest where I work on customer-facing software, internal tooling and automation.

Before that I was a Software Developer at OrcaGroup, where we created critical communication software for alarming and incident notification for organizations. Before OrcaGroup I was a Software Developer at WeMaron, where I developed less critical but not less important software that aims to combat social isolation & promote sustainability.

Outside of my day job, I’d like to spent time playing Padel 🎾, reading books 📚, or petting my cats 🐾.
